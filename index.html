From photo to report in four steps

Capture, check the points, read the findings, share. Swipe through the app.

Everything a posture screen needs

Built for physiotherapists, chiropractors, personal trainers and Pilates instructors.

📸

Guided capture

Back camera with guide lines. The shutter waits until the phone is level within 1°. Import from your library too.

📍

Automatic landmarks

Apple Vision places the anatomical points. Drag them with a magnifier to fine-tune, or reset to the automatic placement.

🧍

1 to 4 views

Quick (side only), 2-view, 3-view or 4-view exams: front, right side, back and left side.

📐

Flagged angles

Head, shoulders, trunk, spine, pelvis and knees, including craniovertebral angle and Q-angle. Out-of-range values are flagged for you.

🦴

Photo goniometry

Shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle, neck and trunk, with range of motion and AAOS normal values.

🩹

Pain diagram

Mark where it hurts on a body map and keep it with the exam and the report.

↔️

Compare & trend

Before and after, side by side, with the change in each angle. Follow 3 to 6 exams on charts (Pro).

📄

PDF reports

Annotated photos, angle tables and a plain-language findings summary. Your clinic's name and logo with Pro.

✍️

Photo consent

Clients sign consent on screen. Its status prints on every report, and it can be withdrawn.

🙈

Face blur

Faces are pixelated on PDFs and shared images by default. Photos inside the app stay as taken.

🌎

Four languages

App and reports in English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish and Canadian French. The report language is set separately from the app.

⚙️

Your thresholds

Metric or imperial units, and editable flag limits in 0.5° steps (Pro).

Private by design

Clients, photos and exams stay on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Landmark detection and summaries run on the device.
