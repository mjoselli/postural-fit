Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? We're here to help.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub with your device, iOS version and what happened. Please don't include client names, photos or other personal data.

Restoring Pro

On a new device, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Managing or cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, or see Apple's guide. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving data to a new device

Client data lives only on your device. It moves with your device's iCloud or computer backup when you restore it to a new iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Landmarks look wrong

Automatic points are a starting point. Use good light, a plain background and a full-body photo with the camera level, then drag any point to the right spot before reading the measurements.
