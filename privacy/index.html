Privacy Policy

Your clients' data stays on your device. We never see it.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Data we collect

None. postural.fit has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your data or your clients' data to us or to any third party.

On Android, the on-device Google ML Kit libraries the app uses may send Google limited diagnostic information (such as the device model, app and library versions, performance and errors) to keep them working. This never includes photos, client data or exam results. See ML Kit's terms.

Data stored on your device

Client records (name, sex and, optionally, height, weight and birth date), exam photos, landmarks, pain diagrams, signed photo consent, settings and generated reports are stored only on your device, inside the app. On iPhone and iPad, the number of clients created on the free plan is kept in the device's Keychain; on Android, it is kept in the app's private storage.

On iPhone and iPad, your device's own backups (iCloud or computer) may include this data, under Apple's terms. On Android, the app is excluded from device and Google backups. Deleting a client, an exam or the app removes that data from the device.

You are responsible for the client data you enter and for obtaining your clients' consent.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to photograph a client's posture. Photos you import (through the system photo picker on Android) are copied into the app; the app cannot see the rest of your photo library.

On-device processing

Landmark detection uses Apple's Vision framework, and the optional findings summary uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Both run entirely on your device.

On Android, landmark detection, background removal and face detection use Google ML Kit, which also runs entirely on your device; photos are never uploaded.

Sharing and export

Reports, images and data exports leave the app only when you share them yourself, to the destination you choose. Faces are pixelated on shared PDFs and images by default, and data exports replace client names with a patient code.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

On Android, subscriptions are processed by Google through Google Play, and we receive no payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is meant for health and fitness professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
