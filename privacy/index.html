Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

postural.fit is built so that your data stays with you. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your data to us or to any third party.

Data on your device

Client records (name, sex, and optionally height, weight and birth date), exam photos, landmarks, measurements, signed photo consent and generated reports are stored only on your device, inside the app. Your device's own backups (iCloud or computer) may include this data, under Apple's terms.

You are responsible for the client data you enter and for obtaining your clients' consent. Deleting a client, or the app, removes that data from the device.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to photograph a client's posture. Photos you import from your library are copied into the app; the app cannot see the rest of your library.

On-device processing

Landmark detection uses Apple's Vision framework, and the optional findings summary uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Both run entirely on your device.

Sharing

Reports leave the app only when you share or export them yourself, to the destination you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is meant for health and fitness professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
